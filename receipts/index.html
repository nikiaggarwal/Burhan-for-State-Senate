The Candidate

Burhan Azeem

Burhan is a transit rider, the cofounder of Abundant Housing Massachusetts, and the Vice Mayor of Cambridge.

Growing up, his family relied on Medicaid and food stamps, so he knows the power of helpful public policy. After graduating from MIT and becoming an EMT, he struggled to find an affordable apartment. So he stopped waiting for someone else to fix the problem, ran for office, and became one of the youngest Cambridge City Councilors ever elected.

During his tenure, he has re-legalized multifamily housing, delivered universal pre-K, and brought greater transparency to local government.

Now he’s running for Senate to do even more: more housing, a faster T, and a State House that actually works.
